Hong Kong style Zhajiangmian (dimcookguide.com)

Serves 2-4

INGREDIENTS

200 g		pork
2-4 portions		fresh Chinese noodles

Pork marinade:
1 tsp		light soy sauce
1 tsp		sugar
1 tsp		Shaoxing wine
a pinch		white pepper
2 tsp		oil

Sauce:
2 tsp		minced garlic
6 tsp		chili bean sauce (doubanjiang)
6 tsp		hoisin sauce
3 tsp		white vinegar
1 tsp		light soy sauce
1 tsp		sugar
150 ml		water
2 tsp		corn starch
50 ml		water
to taste		sesame oil

Instructions

1. Toss the pork with the marinade ingredients and set aside for 15 minutes.
2. Heat a wok or large pan over medium-high heat with a little oil. Add the garlic and stir-fry until fragrant.
3. Add the pork and stir-fry until cooked through and lightly browned.
4. Stir in the chili bean sauce and hoisin sauce. Cook 1 minute until the sauce turns red and glossy.
5. Add the white vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, and 150 ml water. Bring to a boil, then simmer 3-4 minutes.
6. Mix the corn starch with 50 ml water and stir it into the sauce. Cook until thickened and glossy. Drizzle in sesame oil to taste and turn off the heat.
7. Cook the noodles in a separate pot of boiling water until done; drain.
8. Serve the noodles topped with the sauce. Mix well before eating.
