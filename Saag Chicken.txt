Saag Chicken (YouTube)

INGREDIENTS

500 g		chicken thigh - cut into cubes
4 Tbsp		vegetable oil + 1 Tbsp pure ghee
1		bay leaf (tez pata) + 2 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick (cassia bark)
2		onion, 1 heaped tsp ginger, 1 Tbsp garlic
1 tsp		salt, 2 tsp curry powder, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder + 2 tsp chilli powder
3		tomato (pureed)
200 g		baby spinach pureed
1		tomato cut into 4 wedges
2 pinch		kasoori methi leaves
2 pinch		garam masala powder

Instructions

1. Use a food processor to puree 3 small tomatoes.
2. Puree 200 g of spinach with 1 cup of water in a food processor.
3. Cut 2 small onions into thick pieces.
4. Heat up 4 Tbsp of oil with 1 Tbsp of ghee in a wok or pan.
5. Add 1 bay leaf, 2 cardamom pods and 1 cinnamon stick to hot oil/ghee.
6. Add onions with 1 heaped tsp of minced ginger and 1 Tbsp of garlic.
7. Add 1 tsp of salt to soften up the onions.
8. Cook the mixture for a minute and add 1 cup of boiling water to deglaze.
9. Put the lid on and let the onions soften! This may take 8 to 10 min.
10. Use a potato masher to mash up the onion mixture.
11. Add the following spices (in tsp): 0.5 turmeric powder, 2 curry powder, 2 ground coriander powder, 2 chili powder and 1 cumin powder.
12. Add tomato puree and cook for 5 min using medium heat.
13. Add 1/2 kg of chicken to the mixture, cooking on high heat for 5 min.
14. Add spinach to cook at low heat.
15. Add 2 pinch of dried kasoori methi leaves and 2 pinch garam masala powder.
16. Add some fresh coriander.
17. Cook a little bit then add 1 tsp of ghee or butter to finish.
