Dry-Sauteed Mushrooms (Muse)

INGREDIENTS

1 lb		mushrooms, wiped clean and sliced thick
2 Tbsp		olive oil
1 clove		garlic, minced
to taste		salt and pepper
1 tsp		lemon juice or soy sauce (optional)

Instructions

1. Heat a wide pan over medium-high with no oil. Add the mushrooms in a single layer.
2. Do not touch for 2-3 min, until browned underneath.
3. Stir the mushrooms over so the other sides brown, then keep cooking until all their water releases and evaporates, about 5-8 min more.
4. Add the olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic. Toss 1 min until the garlic is fragrant.
5. Finish with a squeeze of lemon or a splash of soy sauce if you like.
