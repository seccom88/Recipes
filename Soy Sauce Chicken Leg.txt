Soy sauce chicken leg (Bo Bo Kitchen)

Ingredients

35 oz		(992g)chicken leg quarters(2 pieces)

Soy sauce mix：
1 Tbsp 		dark soy sauce
1 Tbsp 		oyster sauce
1 Tbsp 		mushroom dark soy sauce
Some 		white pepper
3 cups 		water

3 cloves	garlic w/ skin 
2 sections 	dry tangerine
Some		ginger 	
1 		cinnamon
1 		star anise
1 oz 		rock sugar
2 Tbsp 		MEI KUEI LU (Any cooking liquor can be use)


Instruction

1. Mix soy sauce mix
2. Heat pan to medium heat and slight fry the aromas for about 1 min
3. Add mixed sauce into the aromas and use high heat to cook until boiling
4. Turn to medium heat for cook for 1 min
5. Add chicken leg skin side down into the sauce and cook for 30 sec to allow the chicken skin to absolve the sauce
6. Turn over the chicken leg to the other side
7. Use spoon to pour sauce from the pan over the chicken leg for 10 min
8. Turn down the heat so the sauce only slightly boiling 
9. If the boiling is lower, add heat to make sure the sauce is slightly boiling the whole time
10. Sauce must cover only half the chicken leg for these 10 mins
11. Turn the chicken leg over and add Mei Keui Lu liquor
12. Add heat to medium boil 2 min
13. After 2 min, cover the pan and cook for 30 sec
14. Turn off heat and let the sauce stream the chicken leg for 20 - 30 min (larger leg need longer time)
15. Chicken leg is ready, and it taste great as a cold dish
16. Take some sauce and heat reduce as a dipping sauce. Enjoy!
