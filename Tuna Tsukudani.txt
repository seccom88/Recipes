Tuna Tsukudani (Muse)

INGREDIENTS

1 can		tuna in water, drained
1 Tbsp		soy sauce
1 Tbsp		mirin
1 Tbsp		sake
1 tsp		sugar
1 tsp		grated ginger (optional)

Instructions

1. Put the drained tuna in a small pan and break it into flakes.
2. Add the soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, and ginger. Stir to mix.
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until most of the liquid evaporates, about 5 min.
4. Turn to low and keep stirring until the tuna looks dry and glossy, 2-3 min.
5. Serve warm or at room temperature. Keeps about a week in the fridge - great over rice.

Note: for the restaurant version with fresh tuna, simmer tuna trimmings or loin in the same sauce for about 20 minutes until it shreds easily, then finish the same way.
