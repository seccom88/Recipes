Tuna Tsukudani (Muse)

INGREDIENTS

1/2 lb		frozen tuna, thawed
1 Tbsp		soy sauce
1 Tbsp		mirin
1 Tbsp		sake
1 tsp		sugar
1 tsp		grated ginger (optional)

Instructions

1. Thaw the tuna: overnight in the fridge, or sealed in cold water for about 30 min. Pat dry.
2. Cut the tuna into chunks and put in a small pan. Add the soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, and ginger. Stir to mix.
3. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Cook about 20 min, breaking the tuna into flakes with a spoon, until most of the liquid evaporates.
4. Turn to low and keep stirring until the tuna looks dry and glossy, 2-3 min.
5. Serve warm or at room temperature. Keeps about a week in the fridge - great over rice.
